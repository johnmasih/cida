About CIDA Institute

Training built around the job you want

CIDA is a practical training institute for data, programming and communication skills. We teach with real datasets and real projects, in Hindi, so first-time learners and career switchers can follow every step.

Our story

Started to fix what most courses skip

CIDA began with a simple observation: many learners finish a course with a certificate but cannot do the work an employer asks for. So we built our courses the other way round. We start from the tasks people do on the job and teach only what is needed to do them well.

Today we offer courses in Python, Java, C/C++, front-end development, databases, data analytics, data science and spoken English. Each one includes graded assignments, portfolio projects and doubt support from trainers who work in the field they teach.

Our mission

To make job-ready skills affordable and easy to learn for anyone willing to practise, whatever their background or language.

Our vision

A learning community where every student leaves with real projects, real confidence and a clear next step in their career.
