Before you enroll

Is the course beginner friendly?

Yes — every course states its starting level, and most tracks begin from zero prior experience.

Are classes live or recorded?

Both. Choose recorded for self-paced learning, live for instructor-led sessions, or hybrid for either.

How long do I get access?

Most courses include extended access from your enrollment date, shown on each course page.

Is a certificate provided?

Yes, on completion. Certificates can be verified publicly using a unique certificate ID.

Are projects included?

Every course and career path includes at least one graded, portfolio-ready project.

Is placement assistance available?

Career programs include interview preparation and 1:1 career guidance calls.
